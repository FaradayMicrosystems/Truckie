# Truckie privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Truckie is a route helper for delivery drivers. It keeps your list of stops on your phone and opens
Google Maps or Waze to drive to each one. Truckie has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server
of its own.

## What Truckie uses and why

- **Location (precise).** Used to order stops ("Optimize"), to show how far you are from the next stop,
  and, only if you turn on "Auto-complete on arrival", to tick a stop off when you have stopped there.
  Auto-complete runs while you drive with a visible notification and stops when no stops are left.
- **Addresses you type or paste.** Kept on your phone in Truckie's own storage.

## What leaves your phone

Only what a feature needs, at the moment you use it, sent directly from your phone:

- **Address search:** the text you type is sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io) to find the location.
- **Optimize by road:** the coordinates of your stops and your current position are sent to the
  OSRM routing service (router.project-osrm.org) to get road distances.
- **Navigation:** the next stop's address or coordinates are handed to Google Maps or Waze on your
  phone (or in Android Auto). Those apps follow their own privacy policies.

Truckie does not store this data anywhere except on your phone, does not sell it, and does not share it
for advertising. The services above receive it only to answer the request.

## Deleting your data

Use **⋮ > Clear all stops** in the app, or uninstall Truckie. Both remove everything Truckie stored.

## Children

Truckie is a tool for working drivers and is not directed at children.
